About this map

This map tracks the development of the on-demand retail autonomous robotics market: robots that fulfil food, grocery, convenience and parcel orders without a human courier. It covers six technology families: sidewalk delivery robots, road-going autonomous delivery vehicles, delivery drones, middle-mile autonomous trucks, in-store retail robots, and robotic stores with micro-fulfilment automation.

How to read it

  • Country shading shows the selected metric. The default is publicly disclosed equity funding raised by companies headquartered in that country. Corporate programmes funded internally (Amazon, Alphabet, Meituan, JD, Walmart…) are flagged as “corporate” and are not summed.
  • City bubbles are sized by the number of deployments matching your filters and coloured by the dominant technology. Dashed rings mark pilots, faded bubbles mark ended programmes.
  • Timeline replays the market year by year: a deployment appears in the year it launched and disappears the year after it ended.
  • Click any country, city or player to open its profile. Player profiles list technology developed in-house separately from technology used from third parties.
  • Showcase media: each project card carries a photo or a video of the robot in operation. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons (free licences; the credit link leads to the file page with author and terms), videos are official or press YouTube uploads that open on YouTube. When an image cannot load, a line drawing of the machine type is shown instead. The Gallery view lists every player as a media card.

Sources and caveats

Figures are approximate and compiled from company announcements, regulatory filings, investor press releases and trade press up to . Funding totals are rounded and may omit undisclosed rounds. Deployment years mark first public operation in the city. Use the Table view for the full data set.
